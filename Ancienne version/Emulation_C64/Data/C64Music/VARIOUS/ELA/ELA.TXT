1990/91-Used Megastyle's editor.
Quit Cosine, and got word from Megastyle that they wanted me as a member 
making tons of intro/demo-tunes. I got their editor but they never used ANY
of my music. Their player was based on JB/MON's Stormlord tunes. I decided to 
join my local friends in the Norway/Finland/Germany-based group Antimon.
Most of my music had been used by them anyway.

1991-
At this time I had started to make my own music routine but the scene had
almost died out in Norway, so it was never finished. I made a lot of music
in Henning Rokling's Panoramic routine, but later I found out that this was
the only editor of theirs that didn't have an external player. I haven't
managed to hack the routine yet, but when I do I will spread ca 5-6 of my 
tunes from it + a tune Henning made just for Antimon in it. It's one of his
best and really well planned/executed.

Antimon 1996:
The group dissolved after the Horizon 1991 easter party. At the 
moment I know of only two active members: Me (ELA) and Zaphod Beeblebrox
here in Lillehammer, Norway. Another member lives here too (Edge), but he
can't remember who he lend his C64 to, so he's a dropout! 
We are currently doing some work on PC for a Grocery-store-chain (or sumfin),
and haven't got time for much C64. 

Very last: We put together some old parts and made a new intro to a C64 demo.
This won 1.prize at Gathering96/Norway. Too bad there was only one other
contestant (some Amiga people put up an old part, 7 years old. They didn't
even change the scrolltext. Didn't know how to anymore!). For this we won a
GUS PnP Pro and some Microsoft Junk (C), that was supposed to be easysold.
It wasn't coz they were English and noone wants that in norway. Who cares? 
We are using the unofficial Nashville anyway. And yeah - the demo will be
spread soon ... when we just pull ourselves together and make a release note.
All demos from TG96 were put on a CD - except C64 coz they the organizers have
NO idea about how to transfer C64 stuff to PC/Amiga. LAME! We'll spread it
ourselves, but we are soooo lazy!
